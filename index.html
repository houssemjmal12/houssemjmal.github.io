About

From algorithms to trustworthy AI systems

I am an AI Research Scientist and Engineer and a PhD candidate at Nantes Université / Inria (LS2N laboratory, STACK team), with a defense planned for January 2027. My published research covers federated learning and network security, with hands-on experience in end-to-end ML development and cloud deployment.

My work spans federated intrusion detection on non-IID data, client selection with offline reinforcement learning, free-rider detection and multimodal phishing detection with vision-language models. I also run Mycelia & Rays, a part-time project that applies LLMs and hybrid search to medical document libraries.

Experience

Research and engineering roles

Apr. 2024 – Jan. 2027

PhD Researcher, Machine Learning (Federated Learning & Security)

Nantes Université · LS2N Laboratory (STACK Team, Inria) · Nantes, France
  • Federated intrusion detection (IDS): developed distributed deep learning optimization algorithms for federated intrusion detection on non-IID network traffic, improving convergence rate and detection accuracy with theoretical convergence guarantees.
  • Client selection with offline reinforcement learning: designed a Decision Transformer-based client selection framework for federated learning, reducing computation and communication time; extended it with an MLOps lifecycle using a trajectory-diversity trigger to detect data shift.
  • Free-rider detection (OOD): developed a gradient-sensitivity-based out-of-distribution detection method to identify free-riding clients through anomalous model update behavior.
  • Free-rider detection (statistical): designed statistical tests, applied during critical learning periods, that measure the dependence, innovation and effort of client updates to detect free-riding behavior.
  • Multimodal phishing detection (VLMs): built a framework combining URL features, HTML DOM parsing and webpage screenshots analyzed with vision-language models (Qwen2-VL, SmolVLM-Instruct, Llama-3.2-11B-Vision-Instruct); fine-tuned them with PEFT under federated learning.
  • EU-funded research project: contributed to Di4SPDS, a European project on federated-learning-based collaborative intrusion detection.
  • Peer-reviewed publications: IEEE INFOCOM, IEEE MSWiM, IEEE CCNC, Internet of Things and IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS).
